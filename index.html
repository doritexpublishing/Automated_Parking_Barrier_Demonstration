Automatic Parking Barrier

A step-by-step demonstration using an Arduino Uno, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor and SG90 servo motor.

Demonstration length: 3 minutes 41 seconds Download video
01

Sensor

The ultrasonic sensor measures the distance to an approaching vehicle.

02

Controller

The Arduino compares the reading with the 15 centimetre detection distance.

03

Actuator

The servo raises or lowers the lightweight cardboard barrier.
